Zero Trust Network
FIPS mTLS — identity-based, not network-based
Aligned with Executive Order 14028 and the DoD Zero Trust Reference Architecture. Every request authenticated, authorized, and logged — regardless of where it originates.
FIPS mTLS
FIPS 140-2 validated encryption on all inter-service traffic. Meets NSA cryptographic requirements for CUI and classified workloads.
SPIFFE Identity
Workload identity via SPIFFE/SPIRE. No static credentials. Identity cryptographically bound to the workload, not the network.

All service communication blocked unless explicitly authorized via policy. Lateral movement is architecturally prevented, not just monitored.
